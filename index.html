How to play

MOVE

Say LEFT RIGHT JUMP SLIDE (also “up”, “down”).
Hindi: baayein · daayein · kood · jhuko.
Keys: ←→↑↓ Space or WASD. Phone: swipe.

VOLUME = POWER

Shout “JUMP!” to jump higher (0.8x to 1.4x). The mic bar shows your level.

POWER WORDS (spend HYPE)

SURFBOARD 1 · ride a board that eats one crash
SHIP IT 2 · 5 s boost, invincible, score x2
DEPLOY 1 · shield blocks the next hit
HOTFIX 1 · push the Demo Day clock back
MINT 1 · coin magnet
PARAGLIDER 2 · fly above traffic, collect sky coins
SCOOTER 2 · ride a Vespa, smash traffic
COCONUT 1 · shield that blocks 2 hits
CHAI 1 · slow time down
Keys 1–9 or tap the chips. Say two at once for a VOICE COMBO.

WISPR FLOW

The cream WISPR FLOW bar at the bottom is always focused while you run. Hold your Wispr Flow key, say “surfboard” (or any power word), release, and the word is typed in and fires the power.

TERMINAL GATES

About every 900 m time slows and a terminal opens. Type or dictate the command within 6 s (85% match is enough). Pass: +500 and a free HYPE segment.

DEMO DAY CLOCK

Hits and failed gates bring the clock closer. The first hit is free (grace). A second hit within 8 s, or the clock catching you, ends the run.

COLLECT

Coins +10 · Seashell +50 · Hibiscus +1 multiplier · Coconut restores grace · Surfboard token +1 HYPE · $HHG token +½ HYPE · Blue wing token = free PARAGLIDER flight · High-five fans in the edge lanes for bonus points

BOSS CHASE & BIG GATES

Every third gate is a long DICTATION gate (+1500). At Chapora the Demo Day clock sprints: clear 3 gates in a row to escape (+3000). Gates show your words per minute.

SHOUT-OUTS & ROOFTOPS

Shout “Goa!”, “Susegad!”, “Hacker House!” or “Viva Carnival!” for fireworks and a bonus. Run up the striped ramps onto bus roofs.

TYPING IN THE BAR

WASD, arrows and Space always move you. Press / to type a power word by hand, then Enter.

MISSIONS & SHOP

Three daily missions pay coins. Spend coins in the SHOP on outfits, board and wing skins, and upgrades.
